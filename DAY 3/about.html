ABOUT ISRAEL

Israel is a country on the eastern mediterranean coast of the middle east. jerusalem is the seat of the government, while cities like Tel Aviv and haifa are major cultural and economic centers.

Benjamin Netanyahu is Israel’s longest-serving prime minister and one of its most polarizing modern political figures. Known widely by his nickname "Bibi," he was born in Tel Aviv in 1949, spent part of his youth in the United States, and later served as a captain in the elite Sayeret Matkal military commando unit.A leader of the right-wing Likud party, Netanyahu first served as prime minister from 1996 to 1999, returned to power for a long stretch from 2009 to 2021, and came back to office in late 2022 at the head of a hardline, nationalist coalition. His various tenures have been defined by a security-focused, hawkish stance on regional conflicts, opposition to major concessions in the peace process, and sweeping economic reforms. In recent years, his leadership has faced profound domestic and international turmoil, including massive protests over judicial reforms, ongoing domestic corruption trials, and severe international fallout and legal scrutiny regarding military actions and the Gaza war.
