Sterling Mount Abu is an elegant 4-acre property in the Aravalli range, set amid the virgin forests that surround Rajasthan's only hill station. The 69-room property blends modern comforts with b to stay in harmony with its setting, offering spacious rooms of up to 609 sq. ft. with private balcony sit-outs that look out over the Aravalli hills and forests. It is a considered choice for resort in Mount Abu for family holidays. The resort's rarest feature is its own private entry into the forest, reserved exclusively for guests and ideal for jungle walks in a region known for the green Munia, named for its light olive-green wings. Beyond the gate, Nakki Lake (2.7 km) draws visitors for boating and evening strolls, and the 11th-century Dilwara Jain Temples (3.4 km) reward a visit for their intricate white marble carvings, with Guru Shikhar (15 km), the highest peak in the Aravallis, crowning a day of sightseeing. Whether you are looking for a forest stay during a family trip or a cool, green retreat from the plains of Rajasthan, Sterling Mount Abu pairs genuine Aravalli wilderness with Sterling's signature hospitality.
